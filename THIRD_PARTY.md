# Third-party assets

- Apache ECharts 6.0.0 is vendored as `assets/vendor/echarts.min.js` for offline reports. Its original Apache-2.0 LICENSE and NOTICE are preserved in that directory. Source: https://github.com/apache/echarts/tree/6.0.0
- openpyxl is installed as a dependency; it is not vendored.
- The supplied company Logo is preserved separately in `assets/brand/` and is not an ECharts asset.
- Lieflat Charts report pages were a visual reference during the original report workflow. No Lieflat runtime dependency is included.
